How to play

Tap a domino to tip it the way its arrow points. It knocks the next domino, which falls the way its own arrow points, and so on. Every fallen domino drops into the 7-slot tray, and three of one color clear. Clear the board to win; if the tray overflows, the level is lost. Tap in the middle of a chain to split it: the dominoes behind your tap stay standing. Levels have no timer, and the game plays offline.

Common questions

Is every level solvable?

Yes. All 5000 levels are checked by the game's own solver. If you get stuck, use Undo, a Hint or +1 Slot, or restart the level.

What do the special dominoes do?

Ice dominoes need two hits. Mystery dominoes hide their color until they fall. Spinners turn a quarter turn with every tap. Bombs knock all four neighbours. Stopper walls end a chain.

Where are the other game modes?

Tap Modes on the home screen. Daily Puzzle opens at level 8, One-Tap Puzzles at 15, Zen at 20, Chain Rush at 30 and Topple Tower at 50.
