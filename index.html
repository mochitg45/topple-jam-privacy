Privacy Policy

Topple Jam · Last updated 9 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Topple Jam ("the game") by Inata Sun Soft, published on Google Play and the App Store under the identifier com.inatasunsoft.topplejam.

The short version

The game has no accounts and no server of its own. It never asks for your name, email, phone number or location, and we never receive your gameplay. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob handles to show ads, as described below. The game has no in-app purchases.

Information the game stores on your device

  • the highest level you have reached and your stars for each level
  • your coin balance, daily gift and daily puzzle streak, and progress in the game modes
  • your sound, music and vibration settings, and which tips you have already seen

This stays in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (rewarded videos you choose to watch, and occasional ads between levels). To show ads, AdMob collects:

WhatWhy
Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking)To select ads and limit repeats
Device information, such as model, OS version and languageTo serve an ad that fits your device
Approximate location, derived from your IP addressTo serve region-appropriate ads and meet local laws
Interactions with ads, such as views and taps, and crash and performance data from the ads SDKTo measure advertising, keep ads working and pay the developer

On iPhone and iPad the game first asks Apple's "Allow tracking" question. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you still see ads, just less personalised ones. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change that choice later from Settings → Privacy options in the game. This data is handled by Google under policies.google.com/privacy; see also how Google uses data from apps.

Your choices

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads lets you reset or delete your Advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking lets you allow or stop tracking for the game at any time.
  • Everything on the device: uninstall the game.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Data sent to Google travels over encrypted connections. The game keeps no server of its own, so there is no account of yours on our side to lose.
